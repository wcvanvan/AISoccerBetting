### Caveats

1. **No odds available**: The entire value-identification framework is theoretical without actual lines. Recommendations are directional only.

2. **RM injury crisis severity**: The absence of Mbappé, Bellingham, Carreras, Huijsen, Mastantuono, and potentially Alaba/Asencio represents an unusually depleted squad. My models are trained on data that mostly includes at least one of Mbappé/Bellingham. The proxy sample (games without both) is only n=3 — extremely fragile. If RM field an even more makeshift XI than expected, corner-generation estimates should be revised down further.

3. **Celta home sample**: 9 games is adequate but not robust. The variance in corners conceded is high (2 to 10), reflecting the quality spread of opponents faced. Against RM's quality even when depleted, Celta are more likely to concede 5+ corners than their average suggests.

4. **Score-state dependency**: If RM fall behind (plausible given their form), they will push for more crosses/shots → more corners. If they lead, Celta will push → more Celta corners. Both score-state scenarios favour overs. Only a tight, cagey 0-0 or early-goal-then-shutdown scenario would produce a genuine under. Given both teams' attacking identities and motivations, this seems less likely but cannot be ruled out.

5. **Model limitation**: The Poisson model assumes independence between team corner counts. In practice, open games inflate both teams' corners simultaneously. This means the true over probability may be slightly higher than modelled for totals, and spread confidence may be lower (both teams co-vary upward in open games, leaving the spread less affected).

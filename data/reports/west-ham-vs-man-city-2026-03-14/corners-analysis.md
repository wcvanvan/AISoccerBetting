### Phase 3 — Value Identification

## Caveats

- **Small samples**: City's away PL dataset is only 6 games. The 0% Over 10.5 rate is striking but could be revised by a single outlier result. The consistency across all 14 PL games (and 16 total valid games) partially mitigates this.
- **Underdispersion assumption**: My model uses a tighter distribution (σ = 1.9) than Poisson (σ = 3.0), based on observed data. If this specific game is more volatile than City's average (e.g., multiple red cards, early goals creating chaos), the Poisson model would be more appropriate, reducing the Under edges.
- **Summerville absence**: WHU's best attacker is out. This could suppress WHU's attacking intent (fewer final-third entries → fewer corners) or paradoxically increase them (less clinical finishing → more blocked shots → more corners from sustained pressure).

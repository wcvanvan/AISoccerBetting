### Phase 3 — Value Identification

#### Variance & Distribution Model

Per-team venue-filtered total-corner variances:
- CP home PL: variance = 5.81
- Leeds away PL: variance = 9.44
- Average: (5.81 + 9.44) / 2 = **7.63**

Predicted mean total: **9.0**

Since variance (7.63) < mean (9.0), the data is underdispersed. Negative Binomial requires variance > mean and is inappropriate. **Poisson** (which assumes variance = mean) is the closest standard model but will slightly overestimate tail probabilities. I use Poisson(μ=9.0) as the model while leaning on empirical venue-filtered rates — which directly capture the underdispersion — for final probability estimates.
